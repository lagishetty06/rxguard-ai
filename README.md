## 💡 The Problem: The Polypharmacy Execution Gap
Over 1.5 million preventable adverse drug events (ADEs) occur globally each year due to three systemic points of failure:
1. **Prescription Ambiguity & Nuance Failure:** Illegible handwriting and non-standard clinical abbreviations (`b.i.d. a.c.` vs `p.c.`) fail to translate into structured daily routines.
2. **The Clinical Interaction Blind Spot:** Outpatient care lacks real-time cross-checks for lethal drug-drug interactions (DDIs), food/alcohol contraindications (e.g., Metformin-induced lactic acidosis with alcohol), and Look-Alike Sound-Alike (LASA) dispensing errors.
3. **The 30-Day Adherence & Accessibility Chasm:** Elderly, visually impaired, and non-English-literate patients struggle to manage multi-drug regimens without visual timetables, calendar alarms, or vernacular voice assistance.
